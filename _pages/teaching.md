### ENERES 198/298 Special Topics on Power System Modeling and Markets
**Spring 2026** · Co-instructor · UC Berkeley

A self-designed course addressing a gap in Berkeley's existing course offerings, with an enrollment of almost 30 students, comprising a mix of graduates and undergraduates. My half of the course focused on how electricity markets, regulation, and planning paradigms are evolving under today's pressures of decarbonization and large load growth — moving from the origins of utility regulation and restructuring, through how wholesale markets actually work, to networks and investment, retail competition and rate design, and closing with structured debates on the open questions in the field.

### Energy and Environmental Economics Mentorship Program
**2025-2027** · Mentor · Berkeley Opportunity Lab

A Sloan-funded research apprenticeship program pairing Berkeley undergraduates — with a focus on students historically underrepresented in the field — with PhD students and faculty for direct research training in energy and environmental economics and policy.

Last year, I led a project on using battery storage to improve transmission line operation, treating storage as a transmission asset. This year, I'm developing a project on transmission grid utilization metrics, involving empirical analysis of public data.
