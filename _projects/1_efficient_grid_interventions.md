A research summary on **efficient grid interventions (EGIs)** — a category of tools for getting more usable capacity out of existing grid infrastructure without waiting for new transmission builds. This includes advanced reconductoring, grid-enhancing technologies (GETs), and surplus interconnection service.

The write-up situates these tools within the regulatory framing that currently discourages utilities from adopting them: cost-of-service regulation and the [Averch-Johnson effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Averch%E2%80%93Johnson_effect), which reward capital expenditure over cheaper operational fixes, alongside the interconnection-queue implications of [FERC Order 1920](https://www.ferc.gov/media/order-no-1920-fact-sheet).
