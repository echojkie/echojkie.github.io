Grid planning work using **nodal capacity expansion modeling** to identify where new clean generation and storage can connect with the least new infrastructure — and where the existing system is most exposed to climate-driven stress.

This sits inside a broader research thread on accelerating decarbonization by reusing existing infrastructure and rethinking interconnection policy, rather than defaulting to new transmission as the only lever.
