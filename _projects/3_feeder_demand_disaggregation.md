Methods for taking coarse, system-level projections of future electricity demand — driven by electrification and, increasingly, large AI data center loads — and disaggregating them down to the **feeder level**, using historical month-hour feeder data as the base signal.

The approach draws on hierarchical reconciliation methods from forecasting literature and statistical shape-matching techniques to keep feeder-level projections consistent with system-level totals.
